UNDERSTANDING THE DATA

One Number Never Tells the Whole Story.

These indicators measure different things. For example, stunting and teenage pregnancy describe health outcomes, while vaccination and antenatal care describe service coverage. The percentages alone do not explain why these outcomes occur.
